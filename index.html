Explainable Multi-Criteria Risk Model

Transparent rule-based scoring architecture combining meteorological, hydrological, and topographic indicators.

Governing Formulation 0.0 ≤ Risk Score ≤ 100.0
Risk Score = 0.50 × P + 0.30 × D + 0.20 × T
P = Rainfall Factor (50%)

Normalized from 1h rate, 3h burst, 24h soil saturation, and imminent 6h forecast.

D = Drainage Vulnerability (30%)

Mapped OSM drainage coverage density, historical catchpit capacity, and stress modifier.

T = Terrain Depression (20%)

Relative elevation sampled from Copernicus 90m DEM against Chandigarh's southwest slope.

Data Trust, Provenance & Quality Centre

Uncompromising transparency regarding data origins, API health, spatial resolutions, and limitations

CIVISENSE Data Transparency & Ground-Truthing Charter

1. Weather Integrity: We strictly distinguish model forecast outputs from physical rain gauge observations. Forecast rainfall is never represented as physical rainfall.

2. Drainage Reality: OpenStreetMap waterways represent mapped community vector conduits. They do NOT provide certified real-time siltation levels or underground stormwater pipe capacities. Missing lines in OSM do not prove an absence of municipal drains.

3. Terrain Resolution: Elevation data uses the Copernicus DEM GLO-90 (~90-meter spatial resolution), which represents macro slopes and regional depressional gradients. It does not claim centimeter-level curb or road gutter accuracy.
